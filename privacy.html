Privacy Policy

Battle Beneath the Sea - effective October 27, 2026

In short: the only data we collect is the player name you type to post a score on the leaderboard, together with that score. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Posting a score is optional: you can play without it.

Who we are

Battle Beneath the Sea is a free game made by ebuildy, available on the web (ebuildy.github.io/sub-strike) and as an Android app on Google Play. ebuildy is responsible for the data described here (the "controller" under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).

What we collect

Only when you choose to post a score to the leaderboard:

DataWhy
Player name you typeTo show who made the score
Score and mission reachedTo rank the leaderboard
Date and time of the scoreTo sort and show recent scores

Choose a nickname or call sign. Do not use your real name or any personal information as your player name: the leaderboard is public.

We do not collect your email, real name, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage analytics. The game has no ads, no account, no cookies and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your coins, the ships you bought, the selected ship and your training progress are saved only on your device (in the browser's or app's local storage). They are never sent to us. They are deleted when you use CLEAR DATA on the pause screen, clear your browser data, or uninstall the app.

How we use it

The player name and score are used only to show the leaderboard. The leaderboard is public: anyone playing the game can see the names and scores on it. We do not sell this data, share it for advertising, or use it for anything else.

Legal basis

We process your player name and score on the basis of your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)): you decide to post a score. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking us to delete your entries (see "Your rights").

Where it is stored

Leaderboard entries are stored with a cloud database provider that works on our behalf (a "processor") and is bound to protect the data. Like any internet service, the provider receives your device's IP address to deliver the data; we do not store it with your score and do not use it to identify you. Data sent from the game travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries are kept for as long as the leaderboard exists, or until you ask us to delete them. We may remove any entry, for example a name that is offensive or looks like personal information.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see the leaderboard entries posted under your player name;
  • correct or delete them.

Contact us (see "Contact") with the player name you used and, if you can, the approximate date and score, so we can find your entries. We answer within one month. If you are in the European Union and think we did not respect your rights, you can complain to your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has posted personal information as a player name, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Open an issue on the game's GitHub repository: github.com/ebuildy/sub-strike/issues. Issues are public, so only write your player name there, never personal information.
